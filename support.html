How to play

Open a slot
Drag a crown stamp into an empty slot.
Fill it
Add stamps of the same kind. A full slot becomes a letter.
Stuck?
Tap the deck to draw, use Undo (5 free per level), or tap Hint.
Tap or drag
You can drag a stamp, or simply tap it and it moves to the best place.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
Do I need the internet?
No. The game works offline.
Are there ads or accounts?
No.
