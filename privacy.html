Privacy Policy

Little Post Office (iOS) · Last updated: October 15, 2026

Summary

Little Post Office does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your game progress (levels, coins, Stamp Points, decorations, album, settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No tracking, no ads, no accounts

The app has no sign-in, no advertising SDK, no analytics service and no third-party tracking. It works fully offline. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
